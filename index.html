Shipping & delivery

Orders are dispatched within 2–3 working days after payment is confirmed and typically arrive in 4–7 working days across India. Delivery charges, if any, are confirmed in DM before you pay.

Returns & exchanges

If your piece arrives damaged or you receive the wrong item, message us within 48 hours of delivery with an unboxing photo or video and we’ll arrange a replacement. For hygiene reasons, earrings and nose pins cannot be returned unless faulty.

How do I pay?

Once we confirm your order in DM, we share payment details (UPI or bank transfer). Your order is reserved after payment is received.

Jewellery care

Keep your pieces dry and away from perfume, lotion and harsh chemicals. Remove before bathing, swimming or workouts, wipe with a soft cloth after wearing and store in a pouch or box away from moisture.

What are the pieces made of?

Our designs are gold-tone and silver-tone fashion jewellery. They are not solid gold, silver or fine jewellery, and crystals and pearls are simulated. Ask us in DM for specific material details of any piece.

Ring & bracelet sizing

Choose your ring size when ordering. If you are unsure, tell us in DM and we will help you find the right fit. For bracelets, share your wrist size if you want a specific length.

The philosophy of Auraè

Not made for a moment.
Chosen for you.

Auraè celebrates the quiet confidence of individual style. Our visual world is shaped by sculptural forms, warm and cool metallic tones and the belief that the most memorable details are often the simplest. Jewellery should complement your identity, never compete with it.
